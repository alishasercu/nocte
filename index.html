The story

Made for the hours after midnight.

NOCTE comes from nocte, Latin for "by night". It's a perfume for the moment the music gets louder and the room gets warmer.

Sweet, intense and close to your skin. You notice it when you walk in, and it's still there when the lights come on. No rules about when or how. The night is yours.

The scents

Three ways to own the night.

Amber

Warm, golden, sweet

Wakes up when the room gets hot and gets sweeter with every song.

Noir

Dark, deep, sweet

Starts sweet and turns darker as the night goes on. Still there at 4 a.m.

Sage

Green, sweet, bright

Bright on your skin, for the nights you don't sit down.
