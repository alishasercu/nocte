The story

Close to your skin, long after midnight.

NOCTE comes from nocte, Latin for "by night". It's a perfume for the moment the music gets louder and the room gets warmer.

Sweet, intense and close to your skin. You notice it when you walk in, and it's still there when the lights come on. No rules about when or how. The night is yours.
